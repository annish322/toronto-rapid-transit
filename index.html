Ride Toronto’s rails

A map of the rapid transit network, traced from the City of Toronto’s existing and future network map. Scroll the map, zoom in, and tap stations and lines.

Drag the map to pan and use the mouse wheel to zoom. Tap a station to set your start, then another for your destination. Dashed lines are planned or under construction.
