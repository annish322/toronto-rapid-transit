      About & Sources

      Toronto Rapid Transit Explorer is an independent project for exploring and learning about Toronto’s transit network. Always use the TTC and GO Transit for travel decisions.

      Map

      The network map is traced from the City of Toronto’s existing and future rapid transit network map. Dashed lines are planned or under construction. Dates for projects that are not yet open can change.

      Station photos

      Photos load from Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons when a station is opened. The photographer and licence are shown under each image. Some stations have no photo yet, and none are shown in place of a missing one.

      Station history and design

      Station histories and design notes were written from general knowledge and have not yet been checked against a source for each station. Treat dates and details as a starting point. Per-station source references are planned for the station-profile phase.

      Live train times

      • TTC subway times are read from the TTC’s public next-train feed when your browser allows it. If it is blocked, links to TransSee are shown instead.
      • Times on Lines 5 and 6 can be schedule-based estimates.
      • GO times are not shown in the page. GO station cards link to GO Transit’s own pages, GO Tracker and TransSee.

      Data and licences

      Metrolinx publishes GO Transit schedule data under the Open Government Licence – Ontario – Metrolinx. Check the licence of any dataset or photo before using it commercially.

          Streetcar routes

          Streetcars share the street with traffic, so diversions and bus replacements are common. Check ttc.ca for today’s service.
